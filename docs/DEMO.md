## Submission text draft

**Problem:** Independent artist managers need cities and partners worth investigating before venue outreach. Soundcheck joins cultural-fit evidence, routing, and editable travel assumptions in one brief.

**Technology:** The workflow resolves an act, queries taste and geographic signals, investigates related acts, brands and places, and records source notes. The model chooses tools; deterministic guards handle missing coverage. Source data and interpretation remain distinct.

**Design:** Open a saved plan, inspect evidence, lock or drop cities, review proposed changes, and export a brief. The map, trace, tour and print view carry the plan's provenance.

**Impact:** The implementation supports research. No measured customer benefit or ticket-sales impact is claimed yet. Before submission add the actual participant count, roles, completed tasks, failures, and changes made from feedback.

**Idea:** Cultural taste connects market research with potential support acts, brands, and activations. Add one verified example of a useful decision changed after live-data and user evaluation; synthetic results cannot fill this evidence gap.

**Limits:** City scores come from one region-wide Qloo heatmap and are comparable within a plan, but the published examples are still synthetic. The tool does not know booking history, actual demand, partner interest or availability. Similarity and economics are labeled interpretations.
